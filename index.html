ABOUT LIGEIA

X

LIGEIA is a fashion house belonging to the creative universe of ARLYN LIGEIA, specializing in the design and development of clothing, jewelry, accessories, and author objects.

Its aesthetic identity draws inspiration from the gothic, black hole astrophysics, and retrofuturist movements, integrating historical references, cultural expressions, astrophysics, and alternative visions of the future into a visual language of its own.

The house develops original collections and projects under the creative direction of its founder, Ligeia also known as Arlyn Ligeia, whose authorship is the central axis of its creations.

LIGEIA conceives fashion as a form of artistic expression, aesthetic construction, and cultural identity. Its proposal seeks to transcend passing trends through designs with character, personality, and a recognizable vision.

As part of the ARLYN LIGEIA universe, LIGEIA maintains a differentiated creative identity, with its own aesthetic and conceptual orientation.

FOUNDER

X

LUCA DESCENDANT…

Ligeia, also known as Arlyn Ligeia, is a Peruvian multidisciplinary artist and designer, founder and creative director of LIGEIA.

Her training and career unfold across different creative fields, including film, music, fashion, jewelry, and visual arts. She studied Psychology at university and complemented her education with studies in Audiovisual Communication and Fashion Management and Design. She currently holds a B.S. in Psychology and a diploma in Fashion Management and Design.

Her approach to artistic creation began long before she developed her first professional projects. She grew up in Peru, far from the film, music, and fashion industries, so she learned to build her own creative universe from her interests, references, and personal concerns.

In 2021, she directed her first audiovisual work, a music video for an independent band. In 2024, she wrote, directed, filmed, starred in, and edited her first short film, taking on every stage of its production in full.

That same year, she was admitted to the Master's Degree in Performing Arts at the Pontifical Catholic University of Peru, a path she decided to postpone in order to focus on developing her own body of work.

Her interest in fashion also materialized in her training in Fashion Management and Design and in the development of original projects in clothing, jewelry, and visual art. These disciplines constitute the creative foundation of LIGEIA, a house conceived to develop and materialize her aesthetic vision through author design.

As founder, she assumes the creative, artistic, and conceptual direction of the house, developing collections and original pieces inspired by the gothic, black hole astrophysics, and retrofuturist aesthetics.

Her artistic conception is characterized by a pursuit of inner authenticity and aesthetic freedom. She embraces her femininity alongside her interests and passions, without considering them incompatible elements. She identifies with the femme fatale archetype and understands femininity as a deliberate force, recurring in her identity and in her work.

Her work rejects rigid classifications and conventions that limit individual expression. For her, creation must respond to a vision of her own, without renouncing experimentation.
